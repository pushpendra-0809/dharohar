Historical grounding: the Bihar Museum records a Nalanda terracotta seal/sealing bearing a Dharmachakra and dates one example to the 5th–6th century CE. Museum collections also document Nalanda monastery sealings with the Dharmachakra flanked by deer and inscriptions identifying the monastic community. The game asset is stylized pixel art, not an archaeological photograph or exact replica.
